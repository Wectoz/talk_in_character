# Talk in Character

You type like you always do. Your character says it their way.

Pick a voice, and your chat comes out sounding like a dwarf, a troll, a pirate, a kobold who is very worried about his candle, or whoever you are playing tonight.

> You type: Hello friend, can you help me kill this boss?
> A dwarf says: Hail lad, can ye help me clobber this big beastie?

Made for WoW: Forever.

## What you get

**33 voices.** All eight races, all nine classes, and sixteen factions and creatures: Bloodsail Buccaneers, Steamwheedle goblins, Defias, Scarlet Crusade, Venture Co., murlocs, kobolds, ogres, gnolls, Dark Iron, Argent Dawn, Cenarion Circle, Timbermaw, centaur, naga and Zandalar.

**Mix a race with a class.** The race gives you the accent. The class gives you the attitude. A gnome mage is smug about it. A troll shaman takes his time. Use one, the other, or both.

**Moods.** Calm, angry, drunk or scared. Yes, drunk slurs.

**Ages.** Play a youngster whose beard is just coming in, or an elder who remembers when all this was forest.

**How thick do you want it?** Light is a touch of flavour that anyone can read. Heavy goes all in.

**A phrase wheel.** Hold a key and a small ring pops up at your cursor: hello, thanks, follow, wait, cheer, battle, bye, help. Flick toward one and let go, and your character says a line. If you have someone targeted, they get greeted by name.

**See it before you send it.** A line above the chat box shows what will go out while you type. There is also a box in the options where you can try any sentence without saying it to anyone.

## It will not wreck your group chat

Nobody wants "LFM" turned into pirate. Talk in Character knows several hundred WoW terms and leaves them alone: LFM, WTS, tank, healer, BiS, BRD, UBRS, kiting, wbuffs, and the rest.

> `LF1M UBRS, need healer, PST` goes out exactly like that.

Links, player names, raid icons and slash commands are never touched either. If there is a word of your own you want left alone, like your guild's name, add it in the options or type `/tic keep <word>`.

You also choose which chats it works in. It starts with say and yell only; emote, party, raid, guild, officer, whisper, instance and channels each have their own switch for when you want more.

## Handy to know

- Type `/tic` to open the options. `/tic off` and `/tic on` switch it quickly.
- `/tic drunk`, `/tic elder` or `/tic heavy` change mood, age and strength on the spot. They work in macros too.
- Hold Ctrl when you press Enter to send one message exactly as typed.
- In an emote (`/e`), only the part inside "quotes" is in your voice, so your narration stays normal.
- Catchphrases like "For Khaz Modan!" at the end of a message are off to begin with. Tick the box in the options if you want them.
- Each character remembers their own voice.
- During boss fights the game limits what chat addons may do. Talk in Character steps aside then, so your messages always get through.
- Battle.net whispers are never changed.
- Whispers typed in WIM, WhatTheWhisper or WhisperMessenger windows go out as typed. Whispers in the normal chat box are voiced.
- The voices are written for English. Other languages pass through untouched.

## Goes well with Autocorrect Forever

If you also use Autocorrect Forever by Aurelius Drake, your spelling gets fixed first and then spoken in your voice. You can switch it on and off from Talk in Character's options. Talk in Character also teaches it the WoW terms, so it stops "fixing" strat into start.

You do not need it. It is just nice to have.
