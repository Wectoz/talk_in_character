# Talk in Character

**Speak like your race, your faction and your class.** Type normally; Talk in Character rewrites your chat in the voice you pick. A gnome mage sounds clever. A Bloodsail buccaneer sounds like one. A kobold wants you to leave the candle alone.

For WoW: Forever.

## What it does

- **33 voices.** 8 races, 9 classes, and 16 factions and creatures: Bloodsail Buccaneers, Steamwheedle Goblin, Defias Brotherhood, Scarlet Crusade, Venture Co., Murloc, Kobold, Ogre, Gnoll, Dark Iron, Argent Dawn, Cenarion Circle, Timbermaw Furbolg, Centaur, Naga and Zandalar.
- **Race and class stack.** The race or faction sets the accent and vocabulary. The class adds attitude. Pick one, the other, or both.
- **Moods.** Calm, angry, drunk or scared.
- **Three strengths.** Light stays easy to read. Heavy goes all in.
- **Phrase wheel.** Hold a key and a small ring appears at your cursor with eight kinds of phrase: hello, thanks, follow, wait, cheer, battle, bye, help. Flick toward one and release to say a line in your voice. Phrases greet your target by name.
- **Live preview.** A line above the chat box shows what will be sent while you type.
- **You choose where it applies.** A switch for each chat type: say, yell, emote, party, raid, guild, officer, whisper, instance, channels.
- **Each character remembers its own voice.**
- **Works with Autocorrect Forever.** If you have it installed, your spelling is fixed first and then voiced, and you can switch it on and off from Talk in Character's panel.

Open the options with `/tic` (or `/talkincharacter`). `/tic off` and `/tic on` switch it quickly.

## Good to know

- **Boss encounters:** the game restricts chat addons during boss encounters and similar content. Talk in Character does nothing then, so your messages always go out.
- **Send one message as typed:** hold Ctrl while pressing Enter.
- **Emotes:** in `/e`, only what you put in "quotes" is voiced, so the narration stays plain.
- **Battle.net whispers are never changed.**
- **Whisper window addons:** whispers typed in the windows of WIM, WhatTheWhisper or WhisperMessenger go out as typed. Whispers typed in the normal chat box are voiced.
- **Links, player names, raid icons and slash commands are left alone.**
- **English only.** The voices rewrite English words. Other languages pass through unchanged.
- **Forever only.**

## Optional companion
